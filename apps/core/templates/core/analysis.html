SDG Alignment

SDGs (Sustainable Development Goals) are the UN's 17 global objectives to achieve a better and more sustainable future for all by 2030. This radar chart shows how EU-LAC digital cooperation aligns with these goals. Digital transformation primarily contributes to SDG 9 (Innovation), SDG 8 (Decent Work), SDG 17 (Partnerships), and SDG 4 (Quality Education). The distance from center indicates document count per goal. Hover over each point to see the SDG's name, description, and document count.
