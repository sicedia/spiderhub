{% trans "Qualitative Cooperation Framework" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} This section measures how digital cooperation unfolds in practice — not just whether it exists. Each document in the repository is evaluated by an AI model (GPT-4o mini) against 9 qualitative indicators co-designed under the EU-LAC Digital Cooperation monitoring framework. The AI reads the full text of each document and assigns a score from 0.0 to 1.0 for every indicator, together with a written justification and an evidence extract. Scores are then aggregated here to reveal patterns across the entire document corpus. {% endblocktrans %}

🔺 {% trans "Balance across Analytical Levels" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} This radar (spider) chart shows the average score for each of the three analytical levels on a 0–100 scale. The three vertices represent: Micro (how well individual institutions and actors participate), Meso (how effectively stakeholders collaborate during implementation), and Macro (how strongly documents align with regional strategies and produce lasting impact). A large, balanced triangle indicates that the cooperation corpus is well-rounded across all three dimensions. A skewed or small triangle signals that some dimensions receive less attention and may need targeted improvement. Hover over each vertex to see the exact percentage. {% endblocktrans %}
