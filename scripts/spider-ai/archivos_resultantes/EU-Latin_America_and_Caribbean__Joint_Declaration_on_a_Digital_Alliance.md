## Narrative Summary

On July 17, 2023, a significant partnership was solidified between the European Union (EU) and several countries from Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) through the establishment of the EU-LAC Digital Alliance. This initiative, which encompasses nations such as Argentina, Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Chile, and many others, aims to enhance cooperation in the digital sector, fostering a more interconnected and technologically advanced collaboration between the regions. The alliance is designed to be inclusive, allowing for participation from all LAC countries and EU Member States that have relevant governmental and agency involvement in digital agendas.

The EU-LAC Digital Alliance is characterized as an informal, values-driven framework for collaboration, prioritizing shared human rights and democratic principles as foundational elements. Within this alliance, the EU engages as "Team Europe," which involves not just EU institutions but also individual member states and their development agencies, European financial institutions, and various stakeholders from the LAC region. This diverse participation framework aims to include private sector entities, academic institutions, and civil society organizations, thus laying a robust groundwork for bi-regional dialogue and cooperative digital initiatives benefitting both citizens and businesses.

One of the core objectives of the EU-LAC Digital Alliance is to provide a strategic avenue for ongoing and future digital cooperation. This includes existing programs like the BELLA initiative, which focuses on digital infrastructure, cybersecurity measures primarily under the EL PAcCTO initiative, and various projects related to data governance and technology advancement. The alliance encourages dialogues on a wide spectrum of digital topics such as internet governance, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, digital trade, and connectivity, among others, signaling a commitment to harness the potential of digital technology for socio-economic progress.

Moreover, the alliance emphasizes the importance of creating a human-centric digital economy, advocating that digitalization should prioritize human welfare, with a focus on sustainable development. This entails closing the digital divide, promoting social cohesion, and ensuring that investments in technology yield benefits for all, particularly marginalized groups like women and girls. By building digital competencies among citizens, it aims to improve engagement with digital technologies, thus enabling individuals to participate confidently in the digital economy.

The agreement highlights the shared values between the involved countries surrounding data protection, privacy, and online safety, affirming that rights enjoyed offline must be mirrored online. This perspective also aligns with the European Declaration on Digital Rights and Principles and the eLAC Digital Agenda 2024, both of which advocate for a people-centered and inclusive digital transformation.

To ensure effective implementation of the alliance's goals, a structured framework for ongoing dialogue will be established to exchange best practices related to digital policy, cybersecurity, interoperability, and other topics of mutual interest. It involves a multi-faceted approach to bring together various stakeholders for collaborative action, aiming to converge regulatory frameworks and bridge digital gaps between regions.

In articulating their approach to financing, the EU-LAC Digital Alliance plans to develop a bi-regional Investment Agenda in digital technology, which includes projects backed by the Global Gateway initiative. High-priority initiatives in the near future include the extension of the BELLA fibre-optic cable, creating regional Copernicus data centers in Panama and Chile, and the establishment of an EU-LAC Digital Accelerator. This accelerator is intended to foster collaborations among European and LAC companies, start-ups, and SMEs with aims of creating at least 40 joint ventures that boost innovation and digital transformation within both regions.

To ensure a check on progress and adaptation to the rapidly changing digital landscape, the alliance will undergo regular reviews and adjustments in its priorities. By committing to a constant evolution in response to emerging needs and opportunities, the EU-LAC Digital Alliance stands as a pivotal step toward a more collaborative and prosperous digital future, aligned with shared values and goals for social equity and technological advancement.
